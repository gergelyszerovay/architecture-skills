# Toolshed

> Neighbours borrow the tools they need from a shared shelf instead of each buying their own.

## 1. Problem

Most household tools are used a few times a year, yet every household buys its own. People
who would lend a tool have no easy way to find borrowers, and borrowers don't know who
has what.

## 2. Audiences

| Audience | Need                                        | Excluded need            |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Member   | Borrow a tool for a job without buying one. | Renting tools for money. |
| Steward  | Know what is on the shelf and who has it.   | Repairing broken tools.  |

## 3. Outcomes

| Outcome          | Measure                              | Target     |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| Tools are shared | Loans per contributed tool per month | At least 1 |
| Tools come back  | Loans returned by their due date     | 90 %       |
