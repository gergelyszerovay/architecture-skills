# architecture-skills

> The architecture skills product: the block architectures, their profiles and the documentation and planning skills, published as one package.

## Key Points

- The product is one published package, `@gergelyszerovay/architecture-skills`, whose skills every lab installs and whose scripts the other products run by name.
- The other products of this repository are where the skills are tried; what they learn comes back here as a change to a skill.
- The product's plans are under its `planning/` folder; the plans that changed the skills before it was a product are reviewer2's.
